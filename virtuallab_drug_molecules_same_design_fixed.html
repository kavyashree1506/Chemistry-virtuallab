Safety basics

These are study notes, not a substitute for your institution’s laboratory procedure.

🧤 Protect yourself

Wear splash goggles, a lab coat and suitable chemical-resistant gloves. Keep food and drink outside the lab.

🌬️ Control exposure

Do not taste chemicals or deliberately inhale vapours. Handle volatile, corrosive or toxic substances in a suitable fume hood.

🏷️ Label and store

Keep chemicals in clearly labelled, compatible containers. Follow the current Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and local storage rules.

🚨 If something goes wrong

Tell the instructor immediately. Follow the lab’s spill, eye-wash, shower and waste-disposal procedures; do not improvise cleanup.
